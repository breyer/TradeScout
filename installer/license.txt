TradeScout — Daily Trade Report Tool
=====================================

TradeScout is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or
implied. The authors accept no responsibility for trading decisions made
based on the information it provides.

This software reads data from your local Trade Automation Toolbox (TAT)
database and posts a summary to Discord. It does not execute trades, access
brokerage accounts, or transmit data to any party other than your configured
Discord webhook(s).

Use at your own risk. Not financial advice.
